# SAQAYA Engineering Model & Tech Stack

## Engineering Philosophy & AI-Native Approach
SAQAYA describes itself as an **AI-native engineering company**. Their philosophy centers on **AI-augmented engineering**, where AI tools (like GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or other LLM assistants) are used to amplify the capabilities of senior engineers, rather than replace them.

**[Confirmed]** Their development philosophy emphasizes:
- **Test-Driven Development (TDD)**
- **Performance Profiling**
- **High Velocity & Code Quality**
- **Senior-led, distributed teams** (UK, Spain, Egypt)

**[Likely]** Given their four disciplines (Pilot, Atlas, Owl, Raven), they likely employ a highly structured Agile/Scrum methodology, but with specialized roles ensuring architecture and quality aren't compromised for speed.

## Expectations for a Technical Lead

A Technical Lead at SAQAYA is expected to be a hands-on architectural driver and a team multiplier. Based on their model, the expectations include:

1. **Architecture & System Design (The "Atlas" Role) [Confirmed/Likely]**:
   - Designing scalable, cloud-native microservices on AWS.
   - Defining robust service boundaries and API contracts.
   - Handling real-time, high-volume data systems.

2. **AI Integration [Confirmed]**:
   - Actively using and advocating for AI coding assistants (Copilot) in daily workflows.
   - Optimizing the SDLC to leverage AI for boilerplate, testing, and refactoring to increase team velocity.

3. **Engineering Excellence (The "Owl" & "Pilot" overlap) [Confirmed]**:
   - Enforcing strict TDD and code quality standards.
   - Mentoring senior and mid-level engineers through live pairing and code reviews.
   - Managing sprint execution and removing technical blockers.

4. **Business Alignment (The "Raven" aspect) [Confirmed]**:
   - Understanding the outcome-based commercial model.
   - Translating business KPIs into technical deliverables.
   - Communicating technical trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders (UK clients).
