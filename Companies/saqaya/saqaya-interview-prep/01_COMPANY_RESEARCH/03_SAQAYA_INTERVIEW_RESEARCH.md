# SAQAYA Interview Research & Preparation

## Interview Process Overview

Based on SAQAYA's focus on high-seniority, AI-augmented engineering, their vetting process is known to be rigorous. 

**[Confirmed] Stages of the Process:**
1. **Initial Screening / Cultural Fit**: Assessing alignment with their outcome-based model and distributed team environment.
2. **System Design Interview**: Deep dive into cloud architecture, microservices, and scalability.
3. **Live Pair Programming**: Hands-on coding assessment.
4. **Code Review Exercise**: Assessing ability to enforce quality and mentor others.

---

## Interview Questions & Focus Areas

### 1. System Design & Architecture (Atlas Focus)
Given their tech stack (AWS, Kubernetes, Java/Python) and focus on high-volume systems:

*   **[Inferred] Question:** "Design a real-time, high-throughput system (e.g., a payment gateway, real-time analytics dashboard, or matching engine). How would you ensure fault tolerance and low latency on AWS?"
*   **[Inferred] Question:** "How do you define service boundaries in a microservices architecture? When would you choose choreography over orchestration?"
*   **[Likely] Question:** "Explain your experience with Terraform/CloudFormation and managing infrastructure as code."

### 2. AI-Augmented Workflow (SAQAYA Specific)
Because they are an "AI-native" company, you must demonstrate proficiency with AI tools.

*   **[Likely] Question:** "How do you integrate AI tools like GitHub Copilot into your daily development? Give an example of how it increased your velocity or helped solve a complex bug."
*   **[Inferred] Question:** "What are the limitations or risks of relying on AI code generation, and how do you mitigate them (e.g., security, hallucinations)?"

### 3. Engineering Practices & Quality (Owl Focus)
*   **[Confirmed] Focus:** TDD and Performance Profiling.
*   **[Likely] Question:** "Walk us through your approach to Test-Driven Development. How do you balance TDD with strict delivery deadlines?"
*   **[Inferred] Question:** "How would you handle a situation where a junior/mid engineer submits code that works but violates architectural guidelines or lacks proper tests?"

### 4. Leadership & Business Alignment (Pilot/Raven Focus)
For a Technical Lead role:
*   **[Confirmed] Focus:** Outcome-based delivery and UK client interaction.
*   **[Likely] Question:** "SAQAYA operates on an outcome-based model rather than billable hours. How does this affect your technical decision-making and sprint planning?"
*   **[Likely] Question:** "Tell me about a time you had to push back on a client's product requirement because of technical constraints, and how you communicated it."

## Preparation Strategy for Tech Leads
1. **Study the Playbook**: Mention their specific terminology (Pilot, Atlas, Owl, Raven) during the interview. Show that you understand their operational model.
2. **Brush up on AWS/K8s**: Be ready to draw architecture diagrams using AWS managed services.
3. **Live Coding Practice**: Since they do live pairing, practice coding while communicating your thought process clearly. Expect a TDD approach to be highly favored during this round.
4. **Prepare Scenarios**: Have STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) stories ready that highlight your focus on **delivery speed** combined with **architectural integrity**.
