# COMPANY RESEARCH: SAQAYA

## 1. Business Model Analysis

### Confirmed
- **Nature of Business**: Saqaya is a technology services and consulting firm that builds digital products for external clients. 
- **Focus Area**: They emphasize "AI-native delivery." This means they don't just build AI products for clients; they use AI internally to accelerate the software development lifecycle (SDLC) itself.
- **Global Presence**: Headquartered/operating in the US with a significant engineering hub in Cairo, Egypt.

### Inferred
- **Target Clientele**: Likely mid-market to enterprise companies looking for digital transformation, rapid prototyping, or integrating LLMs into their existing workflows but lacking in-house expertise.
- **Revenue Model**: Project-based billing or retainer-based team augmentation. Their competitive advantage is likely offering faster time-to-market or lower costs by heavily automating boilerplate code and testing via AI.

---

## 2. Competitive Positioning vs Traditional Outsourcing

### Inferred
Traditional software agencies (like TCS, Infosys, or smaller boutique firms) rely heavily on scaling headcount to scale revenue. Saqaya's positioning as "AI-native" suggests they are aiming for non-linear scaling: delivering the output of a 10-person engineering team using a 3-person team augmented by AI tools (Copilot, Cursor, internal automated scaffolding).

**Implications for you:**
- They care deeply about developer velocity.
- They expect engineers to be comfortable with rapid context switching between projects.
- They value automation heavily over manual processes.

---

## 3. The Four Disciplines (Pilot, Atlas, Owl, Raven)

### Confirmed / Highly Likely (Based on naming conventions in modern tech agencies)
While the exact internal definitions are proprietary, we can infer the structure of their delivery methodology based on these archetypes:

- **Pilot (Execution / Engineering)**: 
  - *Inferred Role*: The core builders. Focuses on writing code, utilizing AI tools for velocity, and shipping features. Emphasizes speed, clean architecture, and adherence to requirements.
- **Atlas (Architecture / Foundation)**: 
  - *Inferred Role*: System design and infrastructure. The team or phase responsible for setting up the scaffolding, cloud architecture (AWS), CI/CD pipelines, and ensuring the project can scale. 
- **Owl (Wisdom / Strategy / Quality)**: 
  - *Inferred Role*: Quality assurance, security, and strategic technical oversight. Reviewing code, establishing best practices, and ensuring the output of the "Pilots" and AI tools is robust and secure.
- **Raven (Communication / Delivery / Product)**: 
  - *Inferred Role*: The interface between the engineering team and the client. Project management, product ownership, gathering requirements, and delivering news (both good and bad) to stakeholders.

---

## 4. Internal Engineering Culture & Client Expectations

### Inferred
- **Culture**: High-paced, pragmatic, and highly adaptable. Since they use AI heavily, they likely don't tolerate "not invented here" syndrome. If a tool or an AI can do it faster, they will use it. 
- **Client Expectations**: Clients hire Saqaya because they want modern tech, fast. They expect polished UIs, resilient backends, and transparent communication. 
- **The "AI-Native" Reality**: Code generated by AI can often be buggy or introduce subtle edge cases. Therefore, Saqaya's culture *must* have a strong emphasis on rigorous testing and code review to catch LLM hallucinations before they hit production.

---

## 5. How They Evaluate Technical Lead Candidates

### Inferred
As a Tech Lead at an agency, you wear multiple hats. They will evaluate you on:
1. **Technical Depth**: Can you catch the subtle bugs that AI code generators miss? Do you deeply understand AWS and PostgreSQL?
2. **Architecture**: Can you design a system from scratch for a new client in a matter of days? (The 'Atlas' discipline).
3. **Velocity**: Are you productive? Do you leverage modern tools effectively?
4. **Client-Facing Skills**: Can you explain a technical delay or an architecture choice to a non-technical client stakeholder? (The 'Raven' discipline).
5. **Mentorship**: Can you guide junior/mid engineers to use AI responsibly without becoming overly reliant on it?

## 8. Likely Interview Priorities

Expect the interviews to index heavily on:
1. **Rapid System Design**: Given a client prompt, design a scalable architecture in 45 minutes.
2. **AI Integration**: How do you securely and reliably integrate LLMs into a traditional enterprise application?
3. **Debugging / Code Review**: Finding flaws in code (potentially AI-generated code).
4. **Behavioral / Delivery**: Tell me about a time a project was going off the rails and how you brought it back.
