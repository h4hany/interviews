# Palladium Group: Company Research

## Company Overview & Mission
**Palladium Group** (often referred to simply as Palladium) is a global advisory and consulting firm specializing in international development, strategy execution, and impact investing. 

*   **Mission [Confirmed]:** They work with governments, businesses, and investors to solve complex social, economic, and environmental challenges. They operate at the intersection of commercial growth and social progress, a concept they term "Positive Impact".
*   **Scale [Confirmed]:** The firm operates in over 90 countries, employing approximately 2,500 people. 
*   **History & Structure [Confirmed]:** It was formed through the combination of several organizations, including GRM International, Futures Group, and CARANA Corporation. Palladium is now part of the **GISI** (Global Infrastructure Services & Innovation) family of companies.

## Areas of Expertise
Palladium's expertise spans a wide range of global development topics [Confirmed]:
*   Economic Growth & Inclusive Growth
*   Education and Workforce Development
*   Employment and Community Services
*   Energy, Environment, and Natural Capital (Nature-based solutions)
*   Governance, Law & Justice
*   Health & Humanitarian Aid
*   Impact Measurement & Innovative Finance
*   Supply Chain Management

## Clients, Stakeholders & Global Presence
*   **Clients [Confirmed]:** Their primary clients include bilateral and multilateral donors (such as USAID, FCDO, and GIZ), national governments in developing and developed nations, foundations, and private investors.
*   **Stakeholders [Likely]:** Communities in emerging markets, impact investors, NGOs, and local government officials.
*   **Global Presence [Confirmed]:** Over 90 countries with significant operations in Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.

## Development Finance Context
Palladium plays a crucial role in development finance by acting as a bridge between private capital and global development needs.
*   **Funding Gap [Confirmed]:** They aim to bridge the multi-trillion dollar funding gap in global development and social services.
*   **Capital Advisory [Confirmed]:** They operate an impact investment banking unit, bolstered by the 2018 acquisition of Enclude (formerly Triodos Facet and ShoreBank International).
*   **Mobilization [Confirmed]:** They have mobilized over $650 million in capital specifically for impact-driven objectives.
